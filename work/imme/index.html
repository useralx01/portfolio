This summer's honey

Three honeys,
one summer.

  1. Dandelion, apple and cherry blossom. It sets creamy after a few weeks and spreads like butter. Spun 2 June.

  2. Clear and runny, bright, a little minty. The one for tea. Spun 14 July.

  3. Dark, malty, barely sweet at the end. Only in years when the firs give it. Spun 21 August.

250 g jars. Free delivery in Germany from €30.

We take only what the bees don't need for winter. In a poor year that means less honey, and we sell out early. We would rather do that than feed them sugar.
