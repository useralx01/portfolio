Mobile app

Homing

Homing sells your things from one photo. AI checks the photo, finds the people already looking for it, and the buyer pays with PayPal in one tap.

PayPal AI HackathonTry the app →

The idea

Selling something you own takes a photo, a description, a price and a lot of waiting. Homing turns that into one photo.

What I built

  • AI checks the photo and writes the listing.
  • It finds the people already looking for that item.
  • The buyer pays with PayPal in one tap.
  • Sign-in, payment, shipping label and tracking work end to end.

Where it stands

Built for the PayPal AI Hackathon. The app runs in the browser, on your phone too.
