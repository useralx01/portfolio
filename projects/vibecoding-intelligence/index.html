01 / Overview

What is Vibecoding Intelligence?

Vibecoding Intelligence is a proposed continuous coordination layer for the AgentRoom application.

Persistent reasoning and autonomy service designed to support the AgentRoom workspace.

02 / Inside the project

What it actually does.

  1. Keep continuity above individual coding workers.
  2. Choose and supervise relevant workflows.
  3. Check evidence and revisit goals when plans fail.

How it works

Instead of letting every coding model operate as an unrelated chat, the design introduces one persistent goal, remembered context and supervision of worker agents. It describes how evidence checking and replanning should work; the project documents do not prove that the complete autonomous service is running.
