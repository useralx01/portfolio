What is Fiverr GEO Gig?

Fiverr GEO Gig is a set of marketing materials and example deliverables for a freelance service.

Marketing and sample-deliverable kit for a GEO-related freelance offering.

02 / Inside the project

What it actually does.

  1. Prepare sample reports and scorecards.
  2. Create carousel and story marketing graphics.
  3. Package client-facing example materials.

How it works

Its files include sample scorecards, report previews and social carousel graphics. They show what a possible client would receive and how the offering could be presented.
