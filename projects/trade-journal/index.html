Trade Journal

Rebuilds every trade from a Solana wallet's public history, so you review what really happened.

What it is

Trade Journal is a Solana wallet journal for reviewing what happened across trades rather than relying on remembered entry and exit prices.

What it does

  • Rebuild wallet positions from on-chain history.
  • Evaluate trade outcomes, size, and risk.
  • Inspect coin research separately from source market facts.

How it works

The journal reads public wallet history, reconstructs positions from transactions and compares results against market data. It reports position sizing and trade-review facts; optional coin-narrative research is kept distinct from price sources. It does not need a private key to read public wallet information.
