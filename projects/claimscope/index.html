01 / Overview

What is ClaimScope?

ClaimScope is a roofing-estimate audit prototype for checking possible omissions in insurance paperwork.

A document-review prototype that identifies possible supplement items, evidence requirements and report drafts.

02 / Inside the project

What it actually does.

  1. Analyze uploaded carrier-estimate PDFs.
  2. Suggest potential missing supplements and evidence checklists.
  3. Draft supplement letters and confidence ranges.
  4. Present before/after value estimates and exportable reports.

How it works

A contractor supplies an estimate PDF. The app identifies potential missing supplement items, generates a photo/evidence checklist, attaches confidence notes, estimates ranges and drafts a supplement letter or report for review. Suggested findings are not professional claims determinations.
