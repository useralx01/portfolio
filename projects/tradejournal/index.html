01 / Overview

What is TradeJournal?

TradeJournal is a Solana wallet journal for reviewing what happened across trades rather than relying on remembered entry and exit prices.

A read-only Solana journal that reconstructs historical positions from public wallet activity.

02 / Inside the project

What it actually does.

  1. Rebuild wallet positions from on-chain history.
  2. Evaluate trade outcomes, size, and risk.
  3. Inspect coin research separately from source market facts.

How it works

The journal reads public wallet history, reconstructs positions from transactions and compares results against market data. It reports position sizing and trade-review facts; optional coin-narrative research is kept distinct from price sources. It does not need a private key to read public wallet information.
