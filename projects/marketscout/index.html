MarketScout

Local evidence collector used to investigate markets, creators, and opportunities.

ORIGINAL PROJECT MATERIALPROJECT / RESEARCH
01 Description02 Functions03 Demo
01 / DESCRIPTION

What it is.

MarketScout collects source material for researching real demand, creator campaigns, pricing and product ideas.

02 / EXPLANATION

What it does.

  1. Collect public search and community evidence.
  2. Run preset research workflows for clipping, side hustles, and general research.
  3. Generate JSON and Markdown source packs.

How it works.

Research presets coordinate public searches and available social-media source checks. Results are saved as machine-readable JSON and readable Markdown notes. The purpose is to inspect evidence rather than present an unsupported market answer.

03 / DEMONSTRATION

See it in action.

Available pictures are captured research material, including external analytics pages, not screenshots of an independent MarketScout GUI.

Scope: Research screenshots are source evidence, not a bespoke app interface.
