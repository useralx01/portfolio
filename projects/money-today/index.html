01 / Overview

What is Money Today?

Money Today is a collection of practical outreach offers, drafts and delivery material accessible through a simple launcher.

Local launcher packaging three tested business-outreach paths and their materials.

02 / Inside the project

What it actually does.

  1. Provide reusable offers and outreach drafts.
  2. Organize client intake and delivery copy.
  3. Surface launch actions through a simple local page.

How it works

The HTML launcher organizes three business approaches, the associated copy and the next actions needed to use them. It packages material for manual execution; it does not autonomously generate money or contact customers.
