What is Licht Calls Bot?

Licht Calls Bot is a private Telegram news alert tool.

Private Telegram news-monitoring and alerting bot.

02 / Inside the project

What it actually does.

  1. Monitor official AI and technology feeds.
  2. Filter notable political and crypto-market updates.
  3. Publish focused notifications to Telegram.

How it works

It monitors selected official AI, technology and crypto feeds, filters material updates and prepares concise alerts. The documented scope is news only; it contains no automatic crypto buying or wallet action.
