01 / Overview

What is RealAd Factory?

RealAd Factory is a local workspace for preparing ad scripts, visual briefs and final review packets.

Tools for ad briefs, scripts, visual prompts, render packets and human quality review.

02 / Inside the project

What it actually does.

  1. Generate ad angles, scripts, and video-model prompts.
  2. Organize exported render packets.
  3. Gate output with realism and quality checks.
  4. Support review of final clips.

How it works

A product brief becomes proposed ad angles, spoken scripts and prompts for separate video-generation tools. The workspace tracks render files and applies quality checks before content is considered ready for delivery. Paid third-party generation is not hidden inside the local tool.
