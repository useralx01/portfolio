01 / Overview

What is Lumen?

Lumen is an Electron canvas workspace for arranging real ChatGPT conversations spatially.

An Electron workspace for arranging actual ChatGPT pages on spatial canvases instead of mirrored chat transcripts.

02 / Inside the project

What it actually does.

  1. Place browser chats on freely navigable canvases.
  2. Pan, zoom, and use a mini-map.
  3. Preserve real signed-in chat sessions instead of synthetic mirrors.

How it works

Individual chat pages appear in windows on a canvas. The documented controls include panning, zooming, a mini-map, snapping cards into free positions, and keeping chats inside the signed-in browser environment. The idea is to arrange existing chats rather than imitate them with fake copies.
