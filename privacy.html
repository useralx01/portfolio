Privacy

What happens to your data on this site.

In short

  • This site sets no cookies and uses no ads, social media plugins or tracking of you as a person.
  • Fonts, pictures and videos load from this site itself.
  • What you send through the forms (project questions, call booking, reviews, tester sign-up, support) goes to my own form handler. It runs in my Google account and saves your answers in my Google spreadsheet, sends them to my Gmail inbox, and puts booked calls in my Google Calendar.
  • The site counts visits per day, without cookies and without storing who you are.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colorado Avenue, Suite 1000, San Francisco, CA 94111, USA. When you open the site, GitHub's servers process technical data that your browser sends, such as your IP address, the date and time, the page you opened, the referring page and your browser type. This is needed to deliver the site safely and is based on my legitimate interest in a reliable website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). GitHub takes part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More in GitHub's privacy statement.

Forms: project questions, call booking, support

When you send one of these forms, I process what you enter (for example your name, email address, phone number, company, project details and the time you pick) to answer you, prepare an offer, help you, or hold the call. You get a copy by email; a booked call is also sent to you as a calendar invite. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, because you ask me about a contract or a service, otherwise my legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

The forms are handled by Google Apps Script in my own Google account; your answers are stored in Google Sheets, Gmail and Google Calendar. Google acts as my processor. The provider in the EU is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Data may also be processed by Google LLC in the USA, which takes part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More in Google's privacy policy.

If the form handler cannot be reached, the form opens your own email app instead, with your answers filled in. Nothing is sent until you press send there.

I delete form data when it is no longer needed, unless laws require me to keep it longer (for example tax law for invoices).

Reviews

If you send me a review, I only show it on this site with your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR), word for word, with the name and role you gave. You can withdraw your consent at any time by email, and I will remove the review.

Testers

If you sign up as a tester, I first send you an email with a link. Only after you click it are you on the list (double opt-in). I then keep your name, contact details and answers to message you when something is ready to test. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). Every message has a link to stop them, and you can also just reply or email me. I then delete your details.

Visit counter

When you open the site, your browser sends one short message to my form handler: the page you opened and, if you came from another website, that website's address. It counts visits per day. No cookies are set, and I do not store your IP address or anything else that identifies you. Google technically receives your IP address when the message arrives. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in knowing how often the site is used (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Storage in your browser

The site uses your browser's session storage to remember, for this tab only, that you have already seen the intro and that the visit was counted. These entries are deleted when you close the tab and are never sent anywhere. They are strictly needed for the service you asked for (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict its processing, to data portability, and to object to processing based on Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. You can withdraw any consent at any time with effect for the future. Just write to dretagro@gmail.com.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg (LfDI).
