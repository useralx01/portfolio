Terms

How working together on a project works.

1. Who these terms are for

These terms apply to all project work I (Issa A. Tagro, freelance AI developer, address in the imprint) do for clients: AI agents, apps, websites and local AI setups. If a written offer or contract says something different, that takes priority. Your own terms only apply if I agree to them in writing.

2. Offer and contract

Sending the project questions, booking a call or writing an email is a request, not an order. After we talk, I send you a written offer with the scope, timeline and price. The contract starts when you accept that offer in writing (email is enough). Offers are valid for 30 days.

3. Scope and changes

The offer describes what I build. Changes or new features after that are welcome, and I tell you before I start them whether they change the price or timeline. They only become part of the project when we both agree in writing.

4. Your part

You give me the content, logins, access and decisions I need in time, and you make sure you have the rights to everything you give me (texts, pictures, logos, data). If something is late on your side, the timeline moves by the same amount.

5. Prices and payment

Prices are those in the offer. For larger projects I bill in parts, for example a first part at the start and the rest at agreed steps or on delivery. Invoices are due within 14 days unless the offer says otherwise. Costs for outside services you need to run the result (hosting, domains, AI model usage, app store fees) are paid by you, unless the offer includes them.

6. Delivery and approval

I show you the work along the way. When a part is finished, you check it and tell me within 14 days if something does not match the offer. I fix agreed problems. If you start using the work for real, it counts as approved.

7. Rights of use

Once the invoice is paid in full, you get the rights you need to use the work for its agreed purpose. Tools, building blocks and know-how I already had or use for other projects stay mine; you may use them as part of your project. Open-source parts keep their own licences. I may mention the project in my portfolio only with your consent.

8. AI results

AI agents and AI models can make mistakes. I build in checks where we agree on them, but you stay responsible for how you use AI results, especially for decisions about money, health, law or people.

9. Warranty and liability

Statutory warranty rights apply. I am fully liable for intent and gross negligence, for harm to life, body or health, and where the law requires it (for example under the Product Liability Act). For slight negligence I am only liable for breaches of essential contract duties, and only up to the damage typical and foreseeable for this kind of contract. I am not liable for data loss that regular backups would have prevented.

10. Confidentiality

I keep everything you share about your business private and only use it for your project. This continues after the project ends.

11. Ending a project

Either side can end a project in writing. Work done until then is paid for, as agreed in the offer or at a fair share of the price. The right to end it for an important reason stays.

12. Using this website

This site shows my work. The content is for information only and is not an offer. Calls booked through the site are free and without obligation; if you cannot make it, just reply to the confirmation email.

13. Test versions

Test versions I share with testers are early software. They can have bugs, so use them at your own risk and keep backups of important data. Please do not share test versions with others.

14. Final points

German law applies, without the UN Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods. If you are a merchant, the place of jurisdiction is Esslingen am Neckar. If one part of these terms is invalid, the rest still applies.
